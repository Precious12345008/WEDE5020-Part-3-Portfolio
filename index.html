Our story

Founded by a stylist, built for the occasion

Nqobile Tsotetsi spent years dressing clients for South Africa's biggest stages before opening Pre_Luxx on Minner Street in 2016. What began as a single rail of curated eveningwear has grown into a full atelier — still built entirely around one appointment, one client, at a time.
